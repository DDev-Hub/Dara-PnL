Currency

The symbol shown next to your amounts.

Balance

Set your starting balance and the equity curve will track your account. Leave it empty to track PnL only.

New account / reset balance

Changed account? Enter the new balance. Every trade you've logged stays exactly as it is.

Goals & limits

The target shows as a progress bar on your calendar. The limit marks any day you lose more than it. It's a warning only, it won't stop you logging.

My pairs

These appear in the pair dropdown when you log a trade. You can also add one on the spot while logging.

Backup & restore

Your trades are saved on this device. Back up often, and use Restore on a new phone or browser.

Export trades

Download every trade as a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.
